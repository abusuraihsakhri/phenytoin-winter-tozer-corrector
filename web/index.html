Interpretation and limitations

Educational/research use only. A corrected total level is not a directly measured free (unbound) phenytoin concentration. Do not adjust treatment from this estimate alone. Directly measure free phenytoin when protein binding is altered, particularly with hypoalbuminemia, uremia, or critical illness.

Conventional total reference interval: 10–20 mg/L. The calculator preserves the existing Python coefficients: 0.25 for the revised non-renal equation and 0.10 for the historical severe-renal equation. Different published coefficients yield different estimates; the renal setting must not substitute for a measured free level.

Background: systematic review of correction equations; kidney dysfunction and unbound concentrations; direct measurement in critically ill patients.
